    Room

    Furniture coordinates and dimensions are meters. Softness/absorption and blocked-path filtering are adjustable approximations, not a measured room replica. Hard cupboards do not absorb like upholstery.

    Rig

    Sound

    EQ: AutoEq / crinacle 711 (Pro 2 ANC), RTINGS B&K 5128 (AirPods 4), RTINGS HMS II.3 (EarPods; connector revision unverified). Fit and listening mode change response. Pro 3 numerical correction was not verified here. Preset preamp reductions preserve headroom.

    Start at low system volume. Extra gain can distort; the output ceiling is not hearing protection. Use stereo output, avoid the Bluetooth headset microphone, and disable other spatializers/EQ when comparing. This page cannot control AirPods ANC or read their head sensors.

    Research & calibration limits

    Furniture absorption and occlusion are simplified, not a wave simulation; low-frequency room modes and edge diffraction are not solved. Exact replication needs measured room/binaural impulse responses, source directivity, and personal headphone/HRTF calibration.
