Build your listening room

  1. Choose music. Upload a local track or load a credited demo in Source.
  2. Shape the room. Pick a preset, place speakers and furniture, then drag the listener.
  3. Press Play. Use the full player on Stage or the compact player from another tab.

Start with stereo headphones at low system volume. Advanced mode exposes every acoustic control.
